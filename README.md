# Hi, I'm Esther 👋

### Mechatronics Engineering Student | AI Engineering | Robotics & Intelligent Automation

I'm a Mechatronic Engineering student at JKUAT exploring the space between physical systems and artificial intelligence. 

I'm fascinated by what happens under the hood — from the mechanics and electronics that make a machine move, to the algorithms that make it perceive, reason, and make decisions.

---

### 🧠 What I'm Building

Lately, I've been working toward understanding how intelligent systems actually work, while learning how to engineer them myself — from the data and models behind them to the software that turns an idea into something usable.

My goal is to eventually connect that intelligence to the physical world through robotics, automation, and intelligent machines.
